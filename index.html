Why pack sizes convert to 24-pack "standard cases"

The plant tracks all production volume against a standard 24-pack. Any other pack size needs a multiplier so physical counts match the system.

A 35-pack ÷ 24 = 1.458, so each physical 35-pack counts as ~1.46 standard cases. An 18-pack ÷ 24 = 0.75, so each 18-pack counts as three-quarters of a case.

Type =506*24/30 in any box to do math inline.
